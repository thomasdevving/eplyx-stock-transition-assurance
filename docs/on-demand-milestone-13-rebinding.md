# Milestone 13: stress selection and final execution state

## Saved FastRPC field audit

The three Milestone 12 retry captures selected ten wallet cases each. A comparison by **token-account address** between the population `getProgramAccounts` response and each case's final coherent `getMultipleAccounts` response found 30/30 identical raw token-account bytes. Decoded raw amount, withheld amount, delegate, delegated amount, account state, close authority, extensions, recorded authority, runtime owner, executable flag, lamports, rent epoch and space were also unchanged. The report records both context slots, elapsed time, hashes and byte ranges for every case: [`milestone13-field-diff.json`](../reports/milestone13-field-diff.json). These particular `SourceStateChanged` outcomes were comparison artifacts, not observed token-state drift.

The prior population decoder sorted rows by address, then used that sorted index as a pointer into the unsorted RPC response. Its pointer also named the `{pubkey, account}` wrapper instead of the account object. All 30 selected cases had a mismatched pointer. Population capture schema 2 records the original RPC row index and `/account` path, and offline replay verifies that the pointer's pubkey equals the frozen selected address before reading the original bytes. Historical schema 1 decoding and saved replay bytes are unchanged.

## Selection and execution contract

The version 2 selector freezes the exact account address, selection reason, observed amount, state shape, discovery bucket, authority model, candidate case plan and `FullAtFinalCapture` amount policy before any case execution. A case is never replaced after a later failure or drift. The discovery observation remains historical evidence. The schema 3 case bundle revalidates that address against the single coherent final batch; its Clock slot must equal the final response context. Final source bytes, decoded balance, mint supply, replacement mint and runtime facts are the execution inputs. The resolved amount and case-plan digest are frozen before LiteSVM runs. The result records a separate execution-plan digest binding selected case and observation, final capture, final source state and amount, coherence evidence, candidate program and VM fixture. Offline replay reconstructs the entire plan and VM result from raw capture; saved classifications or statuses are not trusted.

The source mint, token program, account address and valid token layout are identity-critical. Missing or changed identity is Indeterminate. Recorded authority, token state, delegate and extensions are selection or execution relevant; unsupported authority, frozen state, zero public balance or unsupported final shape is `NoLongerExecutable` and receives no VM proof. A changed but still supported shape or discovery bucket is `SelectionStateChangedButExecutable`: execution can prove only the final exact state, while the original shape or bucket loses unqualified execution coverage. Raw data changes that the decoder cannot classify are held Indeterminate. Current raw balance, source and replacement mint state, transfer-fee fields, account data and runtime owner are taken from the final coherent batch. Discovery-time lamport equality is unnecessary for this burn-and-transfer conversion, which does not close the source account or reconcile lamport movement; final lamports remain recorded and bound to the VM fixture. Lamport-only or other non-semantic RPC metadata drift does not by itself cancel execution.

`FullAtFinalCapture` is a preselected policy, not an after-the-fact amount adjustment. The final positive raw balance is converted exactly, without a cap or peer substitution. Discovery and final amounts remain separate. The final amount is compared with the frozen discovery bucket thresholds; this reports drift without claiming a fresh population rank. The selected discovery shape and the shape actually exercised at final capture are reported separately. ConversionStressReadiness credits only exact final executions and qualifies drifted discovery coverage. A successful selected candidate never establishes population-wide rollout, signer possession, issuer authorization or OfficialTransition. The Milestone 12 invariant meanings remain unchanged: a final Proven or Failed VM result is consumed as such, while a case that could not execute stays Indeterminate and the population requirement cannot be satisfied by a sample.

Historical stress schemas 1 and 2 and conversion captures 1 and 2 keep their original strict source comparison and replay semantics. Rebinding requires population schema 2, stress selector schema 2, case bundle schema 3 and conversion capture schema 3; it grants no retrospective proof to older artifacts. The read-only RPC path captures evidence; the candidate program executes only in local LiteSVM, and no funds move on mainnet.

## Fresh FastRPC validation

The final healthy SPACEX and second-asset runs each enumerated the current Token-2022 population, completed recorded-authority inspection, froze 20 non-wallet authority cases and ten wallet stress cases, and executed all ten wallet cases against coherent final states. Both had ten exact Proven stress conversions and a Proven standalone candidate conversion. The underfunded run froze ten wallet cases and each exact final amount; all ten produced genuine local VM failures with watched-state rollback, and its standalone candidate failed as intended. No stress peer or amount was adjusted to obtain these outcomes. The final counts, artifact digests, replay exits and validation gates are in [`milestone13-validation.json`](../reports/milestone13-validation.json).

All three retain OfficialTransition `NotTested`. Healthy and second-asset ConversionStressReadiness and PopulationRolloutReadiness remain `Incomplete`; their block-only gates warn and strict gates block. The underfunded case is analytically `Blocked` and both gates block. Protocol-owned and unresolved non-wallet authority cases remain outside proven conversion; ten tested wallets do not represent the population. All three packages replay offline, including the underfunded failures. The seven named source mutations and restoration result are in [`milestone13-mutations.json`](../reports/milestone13-mutations.json).
